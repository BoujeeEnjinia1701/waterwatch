# BOM notes

- Row numbers match the callouts in `media/exploded.png`, Table 1 of WWT-PRC-001 and the parts in `cad/src/model.py` (`BOM_OF`). Line 20, fixings, is too small to show in the exploded view.
- All prices are indicative USD for one prototype, taken from typical retail and distributor listings in 2026. They are estimates, not quotes; every line has a supplier type. Value-engineering target: USD 300 (`budget_usd` in `project.yaml`, a hypothetical control target, not a limit). Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 319.00 (USD 19 over the target), checked by `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` (WWT-CAL-001, K1). Savings worth trying are in `docs/06-design-decisions.md`.
- TRL 3 changes from WWT-CAL-001: item 9 is a smaller cell (0.19 L of water); item 13 is specified as direct acting; item 14 carries a pressure-compensating flow regulator in place of the fixed restrictor (+$3); item 15 adds an outlet elbow with an open air break (+$1); item 17, a ventilated sun shield, is new (+$8).
- DDR-002 change (2026-09-25): item 9 gains a spare 12 mm port in the lid with a blanking plug (+$1), so that a site that needs the pH probe variant can take one without a new cell.
- Not in the unit cost: the existing tapstand; concrete for the pole footing; a shared calibration kit (DPD free chlorine and pH comparator, stabilized turbidity standards, about $60, estimate); cellular airtime (about $1 to $3 per month, estimate).
- Item 11 is the least certain line. An industrial amperometric probe costs about $1,900, far above the value-engineering target; see the design choices in WWT-PRC-001. The pH probe variant (probe and interface, about $60 to $100, estimate) is fitted only at sites whose pH is above 7.5 or moves more than 0.2 between visits (WWT-DDR-002). It is priced per variant site, outside the base-unit value-engineering target, which puts a variant unit at about $379 to $419 (WWT-CAL-001, K2).
- WWT-DDR-003 (2026-10-02, design for construction): lines 1, 2, 3, 8, 9, 10, 13, 14, 15, 16 and 17 respecified; lines 18 (mounting plates), 19 (lugs and internal plate) and 20 (fixings) added; +$36 in all. The solid mounting plates of the concept, once part of line 1, are now line 18.
